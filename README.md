## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Principal UI Architect** with over **15 years** of experience designing and building scalable enterprise web platforms. My work sits at the intersection of architecture, engineering leadership, and product thinking — I don't just write code, I build the systems and teams that make great software possible.

Over the years, I've led frontend initiatives at companies like **Eli Lilly**, **KPMG**, **JP Morgan Chase**, **Fiserv**, and **Bosch** — delivering platforms that serve millions of users across **EMEA and North America**. I've collaborated with distributed teams in Germany, Poland, and Italy, and I thrive in environments where complexity needs to be tamed into clean, maintainable solutions.

My philosophy: **great architecture is invisible** — it enables speed, scale, and confidence without getting in the way.
